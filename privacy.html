Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 28, 2026

1. Introduction

Mon Outil de Publication Automatique ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how Mon Outil de Publication Automatique collects, uses, and protects your information when you use our content scheduling and publishing service.

2. Information We Collect

When you use Mon Outil de Publication Automatique, we may collect the following information:

  • TikTok Account Information: Your TikTok username, display name, and profile picture, obtained through TikTok's OAuth2 authentication when you connect your account to Mon Outil de Publication Automatique.
  • Authentication Tokens: Access tokens and refresh tokens required for Mon Outil de Publication Automatique to publish content on your behalf.
  • Video Content: The video files you upload to Mon Outil de Publication Automatique for scheduled publication. These are temporarily stored for processing and publishing purposes only.
  • Publication Metadata: Titles, descriptions, hashtags, and scheduling information you provide for your videos within Mon Outil de Publication Automatique.

3. How We Use Your Information

Mon Outil de Publication Automatique uses your information solely to provide the Service:

  • To authenticate your TikTok account and allow Mon Outil de Publication Automatique to publish content on your behalf.
  • To schedule and manage your video publications through Mon Outil de Publication Automatique.
  • To display your publication history and status within Mon Outil de Publication Automatique.

4. Data Sharing

Mon Outil de Publication Automatique does not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is only transmitted to TikTok's API for the purpose of publishing your content, as authorized by you through Mon Outil de Publication Automatique.

5. Data Storage and Security

Mon Outil de Publication Automatique takes reasonable measures to protect your information. Authentication tokens are stored securely and encrypted. Video files are temporarily stored during the publication process by Mon Outil de Publication Automatique and may be deleted after successful publication. Mon Outil de Publication Automatique does not retain your video content longer than necessary.

6. Your Rights

As a user of Mon Outil de Publication Automatique, you have the right to:

  • Revoke Mon Outil de Publication Automatique's access to your TikTok account at any time through your TikTok settings.
  • Request deletion of your data from Mon Outil de Publication Automatique by contacting us.
  • Access the information that Mon Outil de Publication Automatique holds about you.

7. Third-Party Services

Mon Outil de Publication Automatique interacts with TikTok's API. Your use of TikTok is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We encourage you to review TikTok's privacy practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Mon Outil de Publication Automatique is not intended for users under the age of 18. Mon Outil de Publication Automatique does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy for Mon Outil de Publication Automatique from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes. Continued use of Mon Outil de Publication Automatique after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
