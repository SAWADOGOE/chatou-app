Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 21, 2026

1. Introduction

Chatou ("we", "our", "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our content scheduling and publishing service ("Service").

2. Information We Collect

When you use our Service, we may collect the following information:

  • TikTok Account Information: Your TikTok username, display name, and profile picture, obtained through TikTok's OAuth2 authentication.
  • Authentication Tokens: Access tokens and refresh tokens required to publish content on your behalf.
  • Video Content: The video files you upload for scheduled publication. These are temporarily stored for processing and publishing purposes only.
  • Publication Metadata: Titles, descriptions, hashtags, and scheduling information you provide for your videos.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to provide the Service:

  • To authenticate your TikTok account and publish content on your behalf.
  • To schedule and manage your video publications.
  • To display your publication history and status within the Service.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. Your data is only transmitted to TikTok's API for the purpose of publishing your content, as authorized by you.

5. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information. Authentication tokens are stored securely and encrypted. Video files are temporarily stored during the publication process and may be deleted after successful publication. We do not retain your video content longer than necessary.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Revoke our app's access to your TikTok account at any time through your TikTok settings.
  • Request deletion of your data by contacting us.
  • Access the information we hold about you.

7. Third-Party Services

Our Service interacts with TikTok's API. Your use of TikTok is governed by TikTok's own Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. We encourage you to review TikTok's privacy practices.

8. Children's Privacy

Our Service is not intended for users under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes. Continued use of the Service after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

10. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at contact@chatou-app.com.
